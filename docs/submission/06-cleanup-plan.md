## How to delete
Use the app, not SQL.
- **Recordings:** a recording page → **Remove** (or `DELETE /api/recordings/:id`). That deletes the DB row and METAL's managed copy in `data/media/<id>`. It **never** touches the original source file. The row's analysis, transcript, chunks, POV membership and story clips cascade away with it.
- **Stories:** Story Studio list → **Delete**. That removes the story and its exported MP4s.
- Delete the test stories **before** the test recordings, so their exports are removed rather than orphaned.
